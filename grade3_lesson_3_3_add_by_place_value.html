Break Apart to Add!

Learning goal: I can break apart two-digit numbers into tens and ones, add the tens, add the ones, and combine the partial sums.

We will use number bonds and expanded form so every step of addition makes sense.

1. The Strategy

Break apart

36 becomes 30 + 6. 15 becomes 10 + 5.

Add like places

Add tens with tens and ones with ones.

Combine

Add the two partial sums to get the total.

Addie the Addition Axolotl says:
“Keep place values together. Tens join tens. Ones join ones. Then combine the two sums!”

2. Number Bonds Show the Parts

Penny the Place-Value Fox says:
“A number bond helps you see the value of each digit before you add.”
