🤖 Shift a Ten: Flexible Place Value
Grade 3 • Lesson 3-2
0%

One Number, Different Parts!

Learning goal: I can start with expanded form and shift 1 or 2 tens into the ones part without changing the total value.

We will use models, number bonds, equations, and “funny numbers” such as 5 ten 18.

1. What Does “Shift 10” Mean?

Expanded form

62 starts as 60 + 2.

Shift 1 ten

Take one group of 10 from the tens part and add that 10 to the ones part.

Same total

60 + 2 and 50 + 12 are both 62.

62 = 60 + 2 = 50 + 12
Botley the Base-Ten Robot says:
“We are not changing 62. We are only changing how its parts are grouped.”

2. See the Shift with Discs

Watch out: When one ten leaves the tens side, the ones side gains 10. The total must stay the same.

3. Worked Example: 73

73 = 70 + 3
1
Start with expanded form.
73 is 7 tens and 3 ones, so 73 = 70 + 3.
2
Shift 1 ten.
Move one group of 10 away from the tens part. 70 becomes 60.
3
Add that ten to the ones.
3 ones + 10 = 13 ones.
73 = 60 + 13
4
Check the total.
60 + 13 = 73, so the value did not change.

6. Shift 2 Tens

65 = 60 + 5 = 50 + 15 = 40 + 25
1
Start at 60 + 5.
2
Shift one ten: 50 + 15.
3
Shift another ten: 40 + 25.
4
Check: 40 + 25 = 65.
Penny the Place-Value Fox says:
“Each time you shift a ten, subtract 10 from the tens value and add 10 to the ones value.”
