Shift a Ten to Subtract!

Learning goal: I can regroup one ten as 10 ones, subtract tens and ones by place value, and combine the partial differences.

We will use number bonds to show why regrouping works.

1. Why Do We Shift a Ten?

Start with place value

71 is 70 + 1. 15 is 10 + 5.

Notice the ones

We cannot take 5 ones from 1 one, so we regroup.

Shift 1 ten

70 + 1 becomes 60 + 11. The total is still 71.

Subbie the Subtraction Squirrel says:
“When the top ones are too small, trade one ten for 10 ones. You are changing the parts, not the total.”

3. Worked Example: 71 − 15

71 = 70 + 1
⬇ Shift 1 ten
71 = 60 + 11
1
Regroup the first number.
70 + 1 becomes 60 + 11.
2
Subtract the tens.
60 − 10 = 50.
3
Subtract the ones.
11 − 5 = 6.
4
Combine.
50 + 6 = 56.
71 − 15 = 56
Watch out: After regrouping, the tens part goes down by 10 and the ones part goes up by 10. Do not change both in the same direction.
